# Voice AI Healthcare Market Research - December 2024

## Market Size & Growth
- **$468M (2024) → $3.17B (2030)** - 37.79% CAGR
- **Appointment Scheduling** is the #1 application segment
- **North America** dominates with 54% market share
- **90% of hospitals projected to use AI agents by 2025**

## Key Pain Points Enterprises Are Paying to Solve

### The No-Show Problem
- **$150B annual cost** to US healthcare from no-shows
- **$200 average lost revenue** per missed appointment
- AI reduces no-shows by **25-40%**
- Memorial Hospital: **$804K additional revenue in 7 months** from 28% no-show reduction

### Call Center Overload
- Average healthcare call: **6.6 minutes** handle time
- Staff spend **6+ hours/day on phone**, still miss **35% of calls**
- **15% of calls misdirected** by IVR systems
- Each misdirect costs ~**$1-1.65** (100 seconds at $1/min)

### Staffing Crisis
- **10 million healthcare worker shortage** projected by 2030 (WHO)
- High turnover, hard to recruit/train admin staff
- Burnout from repetitive calls

## What Buyers Actually Care About (Priority Order)

1. **Containment Rate** (calls resolved without human)
   - Industry average: 35-45%
   - Good AI: 60-70%
   - Excellent: 85%+ (Hyro's claim)

2. **EHR/PMS Integration**
   - Epic, Cerner, Athena, eClinicalWorks
   - Dental: OpenDental, Dentrix, Eaglesoft
   - MUST book directly in their system

3. **HIPAA Compliance + BAA**
   - SOC 2 Type II certification
   - Encryption, PII redaction
   - Non-negotiable for healthcare

4. **ROI / Cost Savings**
   - Manual call cost: $5.60/interaction
   - AI call cost: $0.40/interaction
   - Target: **$300K+ annual savings** for mid-size orgs

5. **Patient Satisfaction (CSAT)**
   - Target: 4.2+/5.0
   - 85%+ patient acceptance when told "human available anytime"

## The Winning Product Formula

### Must-Have Features
1. **24/7 Availability** - answer every call
2. **Natural conversation** - not IVR/press-1 menus
3. **Real-time scheduling** - book directly in EHR/PMS
4. **Patient verification** - DOB, name lookup
5. **Smart routing** - transfer complex cases to humans
6. **Multi-channel** - phone, SMS, web chat

### Nice-to-Have
- Multilingual support
- Prescription refill handling
- Insurance verification
- Outbound reminder calls
- Waitlist management

## Your Competitive Positioning Options

### Option A: Dental/Small Practice Focus (Like Arini)
**Pros:**
- Faster sales cycle (owner decides)
- Simpler integrations (OpenDental, Dentrix)
- Clear ROI story (missed calls = missed revenue)
- $2-5K/month per practice is easy sell

**Cons:**
- Lower contract values
- More price sensitive
- Need more customers

### Option B: Mid-Market Healthcare (Like Syllable)
**Pros:**
- Higher contract values ($10-50K/month)
- More complex = harder to compete
- Stickier customers

**Cons:**
- Longer sales cycles (6-12 months)
- Need EHR integrations
- Need compliance certifications

### Option C: Technology Licensing (White-Label)
**Pros:**
- B2B2C model
- Recurring revenue
- Let partners handle sales

**Cons:**
- Less control
- Margin compression
